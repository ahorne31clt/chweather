READING THE CARD
Keep the context
with the numbers.
MOS statistically relates model output to weather at individual stations. It is guidance, not a forecaster-issued NWS point forecast, a TAF, or current observations. The latest available cycle can differ by station and guidance family.
The header identifies the station and initialization. HR/UTC gives forecast-valid clock hours in UTC; DT supplies the calendar dates. MEX uses forecast lead hours (FHR). Accumulation and probability rows refer to intervals ending at their columns, not instantaneous values.
Missing codes, categorical thresholds, and row definitions differ by product. They remain unchanged here. Use the official documentation before treating a coded category as a physical measurement.

COMMON ROWS / CHECK PRODUCT DOCUMENTATION
TMP / DPT
Temperature / dewpoint in °F.
WDR
Wind direction in tens of degrees in the raw bulletin. 27 means 270°; calm/variable conventions depend on the product.
WSP
Wind speed in knots.
P06 / P12
6- or 12-hour probability of measurable precipitation, in percent. A blank is not zero.
Q06 / Q12
Categorical precipitation-amount guidance, not raw inches.
CLD / CIG / VIS
Cloud cover, ceiling and visibility codes; consult that product’s category tables.
X/N
Maximum/minimum temperature guidance; read the date and forecast-period layout.
Bulletins: NWS, via Iowa Environmental Mesonet. Airport names: OurAirports, from the site’s published airport catalog. Direct ICAO/FAA-code lookup works even if that catalog is unavailable. Not every airport has every MOS product. No bulletin is manufactured when a station or cycle is unavailable.
