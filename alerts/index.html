Warning

A hazardous event is occurring, imminent, or expected. Follow the protective actions in the official message.

Watch

Conditions favor a hazardous event. Stay informed and be ready to act if a warning is issued.

Advisory

Conditions can cause significant inconvenience or become hazardous without appropriate precautions.

Source and refresh: official NWS active-alert GeoJSON. The page checks for updates every minute while visible, removes expired messages, and shows when the last successful check occurred. A failed or old feed is marked unavailable or stale; it is never reported as zero warnings.

This webpage does not deliver emergency notifications. Keep Wireless Emergency Alerts enabled and use NOAA Weather Radio or local official channels for time-sensitive alerts.
