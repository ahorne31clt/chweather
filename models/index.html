CH WEATHER / MODEL GUIDE

Read the atmosphere.

Display smoothing: choose Native or Smooth beside the zoom controls. Smooth interpolates the colored field while leaving boundaries, contours, labels, and wind barbs sharp. It does not add forecast resolution or change pointer values. Categorical precipitation types remain unsmoothed. Smoothed PNG downloads are labeled as display smoothing.

New surface fields: 10 m sustained wind in knots (not gusts), model 2 m relative humidity in percent, mean sea-level pressure in hPa, and surface-based CAPE in J/kg. CAPE measures parcel buoyancy; it is not a severe-weather probability or a guarantee of storms.

NAM: 12 km forecast through 84 hours at three-hour steps. NAM precipitation type/rate and airport meteograms are unavailable in this version because its feed does not provide the instantaneous precipitation-rate field used by those products.

Regions & sharing: presets zoom and crop the national graphic; they do not add native model resolution. Shareable URLs preserve model, parameter, forecast hour, region, time zone, and loop bounds. Changing models selects the closest available UTC valid time, with a note when an exact match is unavailable. Only each model’s currently published cycle is retained.

Additional fields: 2 m temperature and dewpoint are shown in °F, with city values from the nearest native model grid point. Move over the map or tap for numeric values for every published parameter. These use a 4-image-pixel display grid, so nearby points can share a sample. The 500 mb wind map shades speed in knots beneath 6-dam height contours, with wind barbs using half-barbs for 5 kt, full barbs for 10 kt, and flags for 50 kt. The vorticity map shows model-supplied absolute vorticity in 10⁻⁵ s⁻¹, also with height contours; it includes planetary rotation. Vorticity is lightly smoothed for readability.

Shading: the model’s categorical rain, snow, freezing-rain, and sleet flags, with instantaneous liquid-equivalent precipitation rate. Brighter shades mean higher rates. Multiple flags are labeled mixed; a rate without any type flag is unclassified.

Solid contours: mean sea-level pressure in hPa/mb, every 4 hPa. HRRR uses its MAPS-system pressure reduction. This is not terrain-dependent station pressure.

H / L: selected broad high- and low-pressure centers in the model mean sea-level pressure field. Blue H marks a high; red L marks a low. The number below is smoothed central pressure in hPa. Weak fluctuations and centers too close to the map edge are omitted to keep the map readable.

Dashed contours: 1000–500 mb thickness in decameters, every 6 dam. Cold-side lines are sage blue; warmer lines are muted gold. The 540-dam line alone does not determine precipitation type.

Use the model selector, timeline, arrow keys, and play/pause. Zoom with +/− and drag the enlarged map; Fit resets it. GFS covers the full 384-hour run at three-hour steps. HRRR prefers a 48-hour 00/06/12/18 UTC run, with a clearly labeled 18-hour fallback if extended runs are unavailable. Only the published hours are available. The image labels always use UTC; the control bar can show Eastern time.

These are scheduled model snapshots, not a live radar or warning service. Forecast details and precipitation type can change between model runs. The 1000 mb surface may be below the terrain in elevated regions.
