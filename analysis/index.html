CH WEATHER / SURFACE ANALYSIS

Analyze the surface.

RTMA combines observations and a model background into an hourly surface analysis on a roughly 2.5 km grid. It is neither a forecast nor a direct station measurement. Relative humidity here is derived from analyzed temperature and dewpoint using a Magnus approximation. Wind and gust fields are 10 m speeds in knots.

Station plots show independently reported METAR observations: temperature upper left and dewpoint lower left in °F; reported sea-level pressure upper right as the last three digits of hPa × 10. For example, 132 means 1013.2 hPa. Missing pressure is omitted; altimeter setting is never substituted. The center circle shows reported sky coverage; an × means unknown coverage. A second circle indicates calm. VRB means variable wind direction, not calm.

Wind barbs point toward the direction the wind comes from. Half barbs represent 5 kt, full barbs 10 kt, flags 50 kt, rounded to the nearest 5 kt. They show sustained station wind; gusts are in the station details. Hover, tap or keyboard-focus a station for full values, its exact observation time and the raw METAR.

Station observations are no more than two hours old when downloaded and within 90 minutes of RTMA valid time. They are not all simultaneous. The station-time range and RTMA time remain visible separately. Missing or stale stations are omitted. Station density controls decluttering; it does not change the analysis. Search forces a matching station into view; use CONUS if it is outside the Carolinas. Zoom and drag the image to explore; Fit resets it.

This is a published snapshot, regenerated by the site's workflow. Refresh checks the published files; it does not create a newer analysis. Old analysis times are explicitly labeled. PNG export includes the visible station plots and their time range.
