CH WEATHER / ENSEMBLE GUIDE

Probability, with context.

GEFS: probabilities are calculated from all 31 members (one control plus 30 perturbed forecasts) from a single cycle. The percentage is the fraction meeting the threshold at each 0.5° grid point. These are raw, uncalibrated member fractions. Missing members invalidate the publication; the denominator is never silently reduced.

GEFS is published here at 24-hour sample steps through forecast hour 384. Its precipitation products cover only the six hours ending at each displayed time. The intervening hours are not included. They are not 24-hour rain probabilities or cumulative precipitation since initialization.

REFS: official NOAA probability fields on a native 3 km grid, sampled every six hours through forecast hour 60. The number of contributing members is read from each field and shown per forecast frame; it can decrease at longer leads. This viewer does not invent independent REFS members.

REFS freezing and mixed-layer CAPE probabilities are pointwise. REFS rainfall uses NOAA's neighborhood probability processing, unchanged; it is not the probability that a single address receives that amount. The neighborhood radius is not encoded in this viewer's metadata, so no unsupported distance or point interpretation is assigned. CAPE above 1000 J/kg does not mean a severe storm will occur.

Rainfall is liquid-equivalent precipitation, including the water equivalent of frozen precipitation. A probability for a threshold is not a predicted amount. Strict “less than,” “greater than,” and “at least” comparisons are kept distinct in product labels.

Forecast run, exact valid time/interval, member count, and point/neighborhood type remain visible. These are scheduled snapshots. Refresh checks the latest website publication; it does not start a new model run. Older runs are labeled. Space plays/pauses and arrow keys step frames outside focused controls. Each model change chooses the nearest published UTC valid time and reports when it differs.
