WPC / SHORT-RANGE WEATHER

Amounts and flood potential
answer different questions.

Excessive Rainfall Outlook: the chance that rainfall exceeds flash-flood guidance within 25 miles of a point during the valid period. The map’s Marginal, Slight, Moderate, and High categories describe different probability ranges. An outlook is not a flash flood warning.

Quantitative Precipitation Forecast: forecast liquid-equivalent precipitation accumulation in inches over the displayed interval. The three-, five-, and seven-day products are cumulative totals, not daily rain probabilities. Frozen precipitation contributes its water equivalent.

CPC / EXTENDED & LONG-RANGE GUIDANCE

A favored pattern,
with uncertainty.

Temperature maps describe probabilities for the period’s average temperature relative to normal. Precipitation maps describe probabilities for the period’s total precipitation relative to normal.

A 50% above-normal category does not mean 50% more rainfall, nor a 50% chance of rain on any particular day. Equal chances (EC) means no category is favored; it does not guarantee normal conditions. Read the product’s legend for the categories used at that lead time.

Monthly and seasonal outlooks cover the calendar period printed on the map. They do not provide a day-by-day forecast or predict individual storms.
